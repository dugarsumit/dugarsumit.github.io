Decision thesis

Core idea: optimize for the number of strong future employers reachable without moving house, not for the location of your current office.

For broad tech employment, Bengaluru has several large clusters. For the highest-paying product companies, SaaS firms, fintechs, GCCs, and growth-stage startups, the most relevant geography is the HSR → Iblur → Bellandur → Devarabeesanahalli → Kadubeesanahalli / ORR axis, with Koramangala adding a dense startup and fintech ecosystem.

What the map shows

  • Agara–Iblur becomes an interchange. The Blue Line (ORR, under construction) stops at HSR Layout, Agara Lake and Iblur, and the proposed Red Line (Sarjapur–Hebbal) crosses it at Agara and Iblur.
  • HSR Sector 1/2 is one stop from Central Silk Board, where the Blue Line meets the operational Yellow Line to Bommanahalli and Electronic City.
  • Bellandur, ETV and Cessna get Blue Line stations, but the ORR section is targeted for 2027, so they have no metro today.
  • Harlur has no line of its own; the nearest planned stations (Iblur, Kaikondrahalli) are about 2 km away.
  • Bosch sites (per Bosch's own pages): India HQ in Adugodi, BGSW in Koramangala and at 360° Business Park in Electronic City, and plants at Naganathapura and Bidadi. Koramangala and Adugodi are closest to HSR and BTM; Electronic City suits the BGSW campus.

Geographic logic

Mahadevapura ↑ Marathahalli ↑ Kadubeesanahalli ↑ Devarabeesanahalli ↑ Bellandur │ ┌───────────┴───────────┐ │ │ HSR Layout Sarjapur │ Koramangala │ CBD

The most useful residential zone is not necessarily the geometric center. ORR traffic is the hardest constraint, so being closer to Agara / Iblur / ORR access has disproportionate value.

"vs 3BHK" compares the midpoint of the good 2BHK range with the midpoint of the 3BHK range above.

What stands out: a good 2BHK costs about ₹40–55k almost everywhere on the HSR–ORR belt, so the area premium is smaller than for 3BHKs. On that belt a 2BHK costs about 80–85% of a 3BHK, so stepping up to a 3BHK is relatively cheap; in Harlur the gap almost disappears (about 95%). Indiranagar 2BHKs (about ₹41k median) are surprisingly affordable next to its ₹80k 3BHKs, because most 2BHKs are in older, smaller buildings. Kudlu Gate is the exception: its newer premium societies push good 2BHK rents to about ₹55–65k, above HSR. Electronic City is the cheapest of all, at about ₹26–33k for a good 2BHK. JP Nagar is similarly cheap, with society 2BHKs around ₹34k and much greener surroundings. BTM Layout is the clear budget option at about ₹28–40k for a society 2BHK, roughly ₹10–15k a month below HSR. Koramangala stays the priciest at roughly ₹65k median, and Jayanagar's apartment 2BHKs (about ₹48k median) are scarce because most stock is independent houses.

Deposits & trend

  • Deposits for society flats now run about 3–6 months' rent; some landlords still ask 6–10.
  • Bengaluru rents rose about 8% in H1 2025 (Bellandur ~12%) and 8.6% quarter-on-quarter in Q1 2026, per NoBroker and MagicBricks data.
  • Sarjapur Road 2BHK rents were ₹33–46.5k in Q2 2026 (Anarock), up as much as 76% over three years.

Sources: NoBroker and SquareYards 2BHK rental listings for each area; NoBroker H1 2025 rent report and MagicBricks Q1 2026 rental index via The Realty Today and Outlook Money; Anarock Bengaluru Q2 2026 viewpoint; Business Today on deposits (Apr 2025). Iblur and Devarabeesanahalli have few listings, so their ranges partly lean on neighbouring Bellandur data.

What this changes: HSR Sector 3/4 and 1/2 lead on livability as well as career access. Harlur, the guide's #1 buy pick, scores lowest on cleanliness because of chronic sewage, garbage and road problems on Haralur Road, so inspect the specific project's road and drainage before buying. Iblur is the weakest overall because it sits right on a major traffic junction, though gated societies set back from it fare better. HSR Sectors 5/6/7 score well below the rest of HSR (2.9): they sit next to Silk Board and are the low-lying, flood-prone part of the layout. JP Nagar ties Jayanagar at 3.4: calm, green and family-oriented around Puttenahalli Lake, but far from the ORR employers. Indiranagar scores 3.2: very green with cleaner air than the south-east, but the pub district makes it one of the noisiest. Electronic City scores 2.7: cheapest rents in the guide and well run inside the ELCITA township, but flood-prone, industrial and far from the ORR. Kudlu Gate scores low (2.6): good societies and a metro station, but it sits on the Hosur Road highway with heavy noise and dust. BTM Layout lands mid-table (3.0): cheap and well connected, but flood-prone and noisy next to Silk Board. Jayanagar scores second overall, with the best greenery and safety, but its own air monitor is one of the city's worst for PM2.5 and it is far from the ORR employers.

Best area to rent

1HSR Layout Sector 1/2

  • Best balance across HSR startups, Koramangala product firms, and Bellandur/ORR employers.
  • Good access to Agara and Iblur, which matters more than a few kilometres of map distance.
  • Target budget for a good 3BHK: ₹50k–₹65k/month.
  • Prefer northern/eastern HSR over deep Sector 6/7 if ORR accessibility matters.

2Iblur — potentially even better geographically if you find the right society.

3HSR Sector 3/4 — slightly cheaper while retaining strong optionality.

Best area to buy

1Harlur / Haralur

  • More modern gated-apartment inventory than central HSR.
  • Often better size, amenities, parking, and project quality for the same capital.
  • Still close to HSR, Iblur, Sarjapur, and Bellandur.
  • Indicative 3BHK buy range: ₹1.6–₹2.4 Cr.

2HSR — best long-term location if budget and property quality work.

3Iblur — strong compromise between career geography and long-term access.

Why rent can be financially attractive

A representative example: a ₹2.3 Cr apartment renting for ₹60k/month produces about ₹7.2L annual gross rent, or roughly 3.1% gross yield before maintenance, taxes, vacancy, repairs, brokerage, and transaction costs.

That low rental yield is why renting in HSR/Iblur for 1–2 years can be rational before committing to a ₹2–3 Cr purchase.
